# Chapter 12 Lab: Full CKS Exam Simulation & Time-Management Strategy

**Estimated time:** 120 minutes

## Exercise

Complete a full timed 2-hour mock exam covering all six domains across a personal kind/minikube multi-cluster setup, then review a task-by-task scoring breakdown against a provided answer key

## Key takeaway

Confirm the current task count, time limit, minimum score, Kubernetes version, and CKA prerequisite on the official Linux Foundation CKS page before scheduling, and practice strict task-level time-boxing during your mock exam.
